# Current workflow — physical suggestion box

## Current process

The user-provided description is that students submit suggestions through a physical box and the box is checked about once a month. The exact staff roles and later handling steps have not been independently verified.

1. Student writes a suggestion on paper.
2. Student places the slip in the suggestion box.
3. The slip waits until the next scheduled monthly check.
4. A responsible staff member collects and reads the slips.
5. Suggestions are sorted and may be passed to the relevant office or decision-maker.
6. The relevant office decides whether action is possible.
7. The outcome may be communicated to the student, but this step is not confirmed.

## Handoffs and approval

Student → suggestion box → responsible staff member → relevant office/decision-maker → outcome (if communicated).

The staff owner, review procedure, decision authority and student notification method still need confirmation. This map describes the reported process; steps after submission are assumptions, not verified facts.

## Exceptions to check

- The box is not checked on time.
- A slip is lost, damaged or difficult to read.
- Several slips describe the same issue.
- The suggestion needs approval from another office.
- No action is taken or the student receives no update.

See `evidence-log.md` for the evidence status and `improved-process.md` for the proposed weekly online workflow.
